What a whole house water filter removes from Sydney water

Three full size 20 inch stages with catalytic carbon, which is what actually deals with chloramine. That means every tap in the house and the shower, which is where most of it gets to you.

Sydney's water is safe. It is just not pure. Sydney Water treats it for the whole city, and most of Sydney is treated with chloramine, which is chlorine combined with ammonia so the disinfectant lasts the whole way through the pipe network. Chlorine gasses off. Chloramine doesn't. It's built to last the whole way through the pipes, so it's still there at your tap and still there in the shower.

The numbers are public. The NHMRC's Australian Drinking Water Guidelines set the taste and smell threshold for monochloramine at 0.5 mg/L, and Sydney Water's own quarterly quality reports show all seven chloraminated Sydney delivery systems running between 0.72 and 1.16 mg/L. Well inside the health guideline, and above the point where you can taste it. You're not imagining it.

So a whole house water filtration system here is built for a chemical problem, not a minerals one. Stage one takes out sediment at 5 micron: the dirt, rust and grit you can see. Stage two is a 20 micron low pressure drop carbon block for chlorine, chemical taste, odour and VOCs. Stage three is 0.5 micron catalytic carbon, the media made for chloramine, and three full size housings are what give it the contact time it needs. The good minerals stay in, because minerals are good. The full picture of what is in Sydney water is on its own page.

How does a whole house water filter work?

A whole house water filter is fitted at the mains, before the water reaches any tap. Ours runs three full size 20 inch stages: sediment, then carbon block, then catalytic carbon. Every tap, shower and bath in the house runs through it.

The unit sits on the incoming line straight after the water meter, so the whole house is behind it: the kitchen tap, every bathroom, the shower, the bath, the laundry, the dishwasher and the hot water system. Water comes in from the street at mains pressure, passes through the three stages in order, and goes on into the house purified. Nothing changes at the taps and nothing needs power.

That is the difference between a house water filter and a point of use one. An under sink filter treats one tap. A mains water filter treats every outlet in the home at once.

Whole home filtration for a full house, every tap and every shower

One system at the mains and the whole home is behind it. That is whole home filtration in the plain sense: a full house water filtration system, not a filter on one tap.

A lot of families ring about the kitchen tap and end up putting the whole house system in for the shower, because that is where most of the water actually reaches you, and a filter on one tap cannot help with that. A water filter for the entire house helps take the chlorine and chloramine back out before the shower, the bath and the laundry as well as the glass.

People call this a few different things and they all mean the same job: a whole house filter, a whole home water filter, a house water filtration system, a household water filtration system, a mains water filter. Our reverse osmosis systems under the sink make the finest drinking water in the range. What a whole house filter does for your hot water system and appliances

Everything plumbed into the house sits behind the filter too: the hot water system, the dishwasher, the washing machine, the ice maker and the garden taps.

The sediment stage is the one that matters here. Dirt, rust and grit travel down the street main whenever the network is worked on, and they end up in tap washers, mixer cartridges and appliance inlet screens. Taking them out at the meter helps keep that out of the house. The carbon stages then take the chlorine and chloramine out of the hot water as well as the cold, which is what most people notice first in the shower.

We are not touching the minerals. Calcium and magnesium are good for you and they stay in the water. This is a chemical reduction system built for Sydney, not a softener.

Whole house reverse osmosis, why we do not fit it and what we fit instead

We fit a three stage whole house water filter at the mains rather than whole house reverse osmosis. Reverse osmosis strips the minerals and runs slowly, which suits one drinking tap and not a whole home. For drinking water we fit reverse osmosis under the sink and remineralise it.

There is a second reason, and it is the one people do not expect. A reverse osmosis membrane rejects by size and charge, and monochloramine is a small uncharged molecule, so it goes straight through. The membrane doesn't take chloramine out. The carbon does. A whole house reverse osmosis system would strip the minerals from every tap, run the whole home slowly, and still leave the chloramine to the carbon stage in front of it.

Three full size stages of catalytic carbon at the mains do the whole house job, and a reverse osmosis for drinking water system under the kitchen sink does the glass. Plenty of homes have both. If you are weighing up whole house or under sink, that page walks through it.

How does a whole house water filter work?

A whole house water filter is plumbed into the mains at your water meter, so every drop entering the home passes through it before it reaches a tap. Ours runs three full size 20 inch stages: sediment, carbon block, then catalytic carbon. Nothing changes at the taps.

Does a whole house water filter remove chloramine?

It helps reduce it, and that is what the third stage is for. Chloramine needs catalytic carbon and contact time, which is why we fit three full size housings rather than a small unit. Most of Sydney is chloraminated, so on this system it is the stage that matters most.

Does a whole house water filter remove fluoride?

No, and no carbon based whole house filter does. Fluoride is a dissolved ion and it passes straight through carbon. If that is what you are after, a reverse osmosis system under the kitchen sink is the one that helps remove it, and plenty of homes run both.

Do whole house water filters reduce water pressure?

On most homes you will not notice a difference at the tap. The three full size 20 inch high flow housings and the low pressure drop carbon block are exactly why we fit this unit at the mains and not a smaller one. The gauges on top show the pressure across the stages at every service.

Will a whole house water filter change how the water tastes?

Yes, and it is usually the first thing people say. Chlorine and chloramine are what you taste and smell in Sydney's water, and the carbon stages help take them out. The minerals stay in, so it does not taste flat. It tastes like water.

How long does a whole house water filter installation take?

About two hours on a standard Sydney home. The water is off for a short while and back on before Jean-Paul leaves, and there is no return visit. Most installations are booked within the week of the quote.

Do you need a licensed plumber to install a whole house water filter in NSW?

Yes. Anything connected to the mains water supply is plumbing work and has to be done by a licensed plumber. Jean-Paul Barbar holds NSW plumbing licence 461511C and does every install himself.

Does a whole house water filter need power or a drain?

Neither. It runs on your mains pressure, so there is no power point, no drain line and no waste water. That is one of the differences between a whole house filter and a reverse osmosis system, which does need a drain.

What size whole house water filter does my home need?

The three stage 20 inch system suits a normal Sydney household, which is most homes we see. On a larger home with several bathrooms running at once, or a shared supply, we check the flow rate and the meter at the free assessment and tell you before you book.

Can a whole house water filter run on rainwater or tank water?

This system is configured for Sydney mains water. Tank water is a different job: it needs its own sediment stages and usually an ultraviolet stage as well. Give us a call and we will tell you honestly what your supply needs.

Do you fit whole house reverse osmosis?

We fit a three stage whole house system at the mains rather than whole house reverse osmosis. Reverse osmosis strips the minerals and runs slowly, which suits one drinking tap and not a whole home. For drinking water we fit a reverse osmosis system under the sink and remineralise it.

Can I have a whole house water filter and a drinking water filter?

Yes, and it is the setup a lot of families end up with. The whole house filter handles every tap, the shower and the appliances, and an under sink reverse osmosis system makes the drinking water at the kitchen. Ask for both on the quote and we price them together.
