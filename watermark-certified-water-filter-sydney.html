What WaterMark means

One mark. Three things it proves.

Australia's mandatory certification for plumbing products, run by the Australian Building Codes Board. A plumbing approval, not a health claim.

  1. Tested to the Australian standard.

    AS 3497:2021 or the WaterMark technical specification, by an accredited body. Never by the people who sell it.

  2. Listed on the government register.

    A certificate number, every model it covers and an expiry date, public for anyone to check. Yours is in the finder above.

  3. Installed by a licensed plumber.

    Anything connected to drinking water in NSW has to be, so the two always go together.

What is WaterMark certification on a water filter?

WaterMark is Australia's mandatory certification for plumbing products, run by the Australian Building Codes Board. Anything connected to a building's drinking water supply has to carry it, and it has to be installed by a licensed plumber. A WaterMark certified water filter has been tested by an accredited body to AS 3497:2021 and is listed on the Australian Building Codes Board register. Every reverse osmosis, under sink and whole house system Filters For You installs in Sydney is on that register, under certificate 23247, 22780 or 080113.

Are the water filters Filters For You installs WaterMark certified?

Yes. Every reverse osmosis, under sink and whole house system we install is on the government register. Pure Flow, Pure Essential and Pure Premium are on certificate 23247, active, valid to 3 October 2028. Pure Luxe, our smart reverse osmosis system, is on certificate 22780, active, valid to 14 November 2028. Pure Home and Pure Home Reserve, our whole house filtration systems, are on certificate 080113, every model active, valid to 3 October 2030. Choose your system in the finder on this page and its certificate opens on the government register.

Which certificate covers my system?

The three certificates are not interchangeable. 23247 is the AS 3497:2021 certificate for Pure Flow, Pure Essential and Pure Premium, the standard for drinking water treatment systems, design and performance, installed in accordance with the Plumbing Code of Australia. It was issued by Pro-Switch Pty Ltd, a JAS-ANZ accredited conformity assessment body, first in December 2016. Its schedule lists the sediment and carbon block cartridges, the granular carbon, the 50 GPD reverse osmosis membrane, the alkaline and hydrogen rich alkaline cartridges, the housings and manifolds they sit in, and the quick change inline filter in Pure Flow. 22780 is the certificate for Pure Luxe, our smart reverse osmosis system with its live purity display, issued by IAPMO Oceania under WMTS-103:2016, the WaterMark technical specification for water treatment systems, first certified in November 2018. 080113 is the AS 3497:2021 certificate for Pure Home and Pure Home Reserve, our whole house systems, issued by CMI Certification Pty Ltd, first in October 2025. Ask, and we point you at the exact line on the register for the system in your kitchen.

Is NSF certification the same as WaterMark?

No, they answer different questions, and a good system answers both. WaterMark is the Australian plumbing approval: the product is safe to connect to drinking water and may legally be installed. NSF/ANSI standards are international performance standards for filter media: 42 for chlorine, taste and odour, 53 for health related contaminants such as lead and cysts, and 58 for reverse osmosis systems and their membranes. The carbon blocks, sediment cartridges and membranes in Pure Essential and Pure Premium are manufactured in compliance with NSF/ANSI 42, 53 and 58. That is the manufacturer's statement about how the media is built, and we describe it exactly that way. NSF is not an Australian legal requirement and never replaces WaterMark.

Does WaterMark certification guarantee the water quality?

WaterMark certifies the product: its materials, its design and that it will not contaminate the drinking water supply. The mark on the housing means the product is fit to connect to the mains. It is a plumbing approval, not a health claim, and we never present it as one. What the system does to the water is shown separately. The independent laboratory report on this page measures tap water beside reverse osmosis water across more than 40 parameters, and the hydrogen alkaline cartridge specification is the supplier's own data across the life of the cartridge, labelled that way.

How do I check a WaterMark certificate myself?

Open the Australian Building Codes Board WaterMark product search at watermark.abcb.gov.au and enter the certificate number. The certificates behind our systems are 23247, 22780 and 080113, and the links on this page open those exact entries. The register shows the certifier, the standard, every model listed and the expiry date. If a product is not on that register, it is not WaterMark certified, whatever the box says. Comparing a system from anywhere else? Ask for its certificate number and look it up the same way.

Who installs a WaterMark certified water filter in Sydney?

A licensed plumber has to. In NSW the Home Building Act requires the licence number on all advertising, on every contract and on every quote, and it can be checked on the NSW Government's Verify NSW register. Filters For You installations are carried out by licensed plumbers across Greater Sydney from Croydon Park. Our founder, Jean-Paul Barbar, holds NSW plumbing licence 461511C, current to March 2027.

What does the certificate of analysis actually show?

Laboratory report B767408 compares a tap water sample with the same water after reverse osmosis and after the alkaline stages, across more than 40 parameters: fluoride, dissolved solids, chlorine, hardness and a metals panel of lead, arsenic, cadmium, chromium, copper, nickel and antimony, each measured before and after. Fluoride measured 0.74 mg/L in the tap water and under 0.05 mg/L after reverse osmosis. It was run on the laboratory's own supply as a demonstration of what the membrane does, so it is not a test of Sydney water, and it is published so you can read it in full. The numbers we quote are the numbers you can read.
