What The Mixers Do

Pull-Out Hose Or Vegie Sprayer

Two functions worth understanding before you choose, because they are the difference between a $500 mixer and a $1,050 one.

The pull-out hose

Luxe 3-way mixers hide a flexible hose inside the spout. Pull it out to reach every corner of the sink, rinse an oversized pot, or fill a vase on the bench. It clicks back into place when you are done.

  • Hot, cold and filtered water from one tap
  • Frees the bench space a second tap would take
  • Five finishes, $850 stainless or $1,050 in colour

The vegie sprayer

A button on the spray head toggles between a gentle aerated stream and a strong spray. It makes light work of washing produce and cleaning the sink, and the same tap still pours filtered water for drinking and cooking.

  • Switchable stream and spray pull-out head
  • Filtered drinking water from the same spout
  • Magnetic dock and button activated filtered water on the Stainless Magnetic
  • Six to choose from, $600 to $750
