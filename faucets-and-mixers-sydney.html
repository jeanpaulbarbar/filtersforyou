Good to know

A few finishing details.

What's the difference between a mixer and a filter tap?

A three way mixer replaces your existing kitchen mixer and provides hot, cold and filtered water from one tap. A dedicated filter tap provides filtered water only and sits beside the mixer you already have.

Does the tap purify the water itself?

The filtration system sits under the sink. The tap delivers its water. Pair it with one of the three reverse osmosis systems above, or explore reverse osmosis in Sydney in full.

Can I match my kitchen's finish?

Choose from the finishes shown for each model in the studio. We confirm your model, finish and availability when we prepare your quote.

Will it fit my existing sink?

We check the existing hole, the bench space, the cabinet clearance and the water connections. Send a photo with your enquiry so we can confirm what suits before installation.

Can you fit a tap to my existing filter?

We assess the existing system and its connections first. Standalone tap replacements are quoted individually. The prices on this page are for fitting the tap with a filtration system.
