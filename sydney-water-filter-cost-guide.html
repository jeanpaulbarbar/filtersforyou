Water filter installation in Sydney costs $760 for Pure Flow, $1,090 to $1,990 for reverse osmosis, and from $2,950 for whole house filtration. Installation by a licensed plumber is in every price, with the system and GST. No separate call out or install fee.

Can I have chilled filtered water?

Yes. The undersink chiller is an add on, $1,050 installed with a system. It chills filtered water on its way to the tap, to around 10 degrees, with about eight glasses ready to pour. It needs a power point under the sink. No yearly servicing. It carries the maker's 3 year warranty on parts and labour, and is not part of the lifetime warranty. See the chiller.
