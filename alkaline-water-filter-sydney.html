What Is an Alkaline Water Filter?

An alkaline water filter is a reverse osmosis system with an additional remineralisation stage added after the RO membrane. Standard reverse osmosis helps remove up to 99% of dissolved contaminants from your tap water, but the process also strips naturally occurring beneficial minerals like calcium, magnesium and potassium. The filtered water produced by a basic RO system is slightly acidic as a result.

An alkaline mineral stage solves this. After water passes through the RO membrane, it flows through a mineral cartridge that puts minerals back in. In Pure Premium that is magnesium and potassium, with dissolved hydrogen, and it raises alkalinity. In Pure Luxe, calcite adds calcium back in. The result is water that has been purified and then remineralised, for a clean, alkaline drinking water.

Alkaline water filters install under the kitchen sink, just like a standard RO system, and connect to a dedicated filtered water tap on your benchtop. We install alkaline systems across Sydney, call 0430 546 749 to book or get a quote.

What Are the Benefits of Alkaline Water?

Here is what an alkaline system gives you:

  • Improved taste: Most customers find alkaline remineralised water tastes noticeably better than plain reverse osmosis water. The added minerals reduce the flat taste of purified water.
  • Minerals back in: Pure Premium adds magnesium and potassium back in, and Pure Luxe adds calcium. Minerals are good, and these are the ones reverse osmosis takes out.
  • Hydrogen rich: Pure Premium's mineral stage also introduces dissolved hydrogen, so it is hydrogen rich alkaline water on tap.
  • Purified first: The reverse osmosis membrane helps remove up to 99% of dissolved contaminants, including dissolved metals, dissolved salts, PFAS and microplastics, as published by the manufacturer.

Jean-Paul recommends alkaline systems for the taste and the minerals, on top of the same purification as any of our reverse osmosis systems. And it means no more bottled water coming through the front door.

How Alkaline Remineralisation Works

When water passes through a reverse osmosis membrane, the 0.0001 micron membrane helps reduce dissolved contaminants, including dissolved metals, dissolved salts and PFAS, but it also takes out naturally occurring minerals. The purified water that comes off the membrane holds very little dissolved solids, and it is slightly acidic.

The alkaline cartridge sits after the membrane. In Pure Premium it is a mineral stage that adds magnesium and potassium, raises alkalinity and introduces dissolved hydrogen, and it holds a high pH for a full twelve months. In Pure Luxe, calcite adds calcium and helps balance the pH after purification. This is remineralisation: the good minerals go back in.

The cartridges are changed once a year, and you choose how. Pure Premium: DIY kit $250, Professional yearly service $390, or Filtered for Life $650 a year. Pure Luxe: DIY kit $270, Professional yearly service $420, or Filtered for Life $650 a year. Filtered for Life is everything the yearly service does, plus the lifetime warranty, paid once a year at the service. See service plans for the detail. Installation Process

Installing an alkaline RO system follows the same process as a standard RO installation:

  • Your licensed plumber arrives at the agreed time
  • Connects the system to the cold water supply under your kitchen sink
  • Routes tubing to the drain for wastewater disposal
  • Installs the dedicated filtered water tap on your sink benchtop (hole drilling included)
  • Fills the pressurised storage tank and tests water quality with a TDS meter
  • Walks you through the system operation and cartridge replacement process

Installation typically takes 2 to 3 hours. Your kitchen is left clean and tidy. Every system is covered for life while you stay on the Filtered for Life plan. See full pricing and compare against our full range at the reverse osmosis page.
