1 Services

Filters For You (operated by Tapped Out Pty Ltd, ABN 81 672 101 674, Plumbing Licence 461511C) supplies and installs water filtration systems for residential and commercial properties within approximately 25km of Sydney CBD.

All installations are subject to a site suitability assessment. We reserve the right to decline work where site conditions make safe or compliant installation impractical.

2 Fixed Price Packages

Our published prices apply to standard installations only. Additional costs may apply for:

  • Cabinetry modifications or carpentry work
  • Additional plumbing runs beyond standard scope
  • Electrical work required for system power
  • Restricted or difficult access conditions
  • Non-standard fittings, thread types, or connections
  • Non-compliant existing plumbing that must be rectified before installation

Any variation to the fixed price will always be discussed and approved by you before any additional work is carried out. No surprises.

3 Site Suitability

The customer is responsible for ensuring:

  • Adequate cabinet space or installation area is available
  • Cabinet or mounting structure is in sound condition
  • Reasonable access to plumbing connections is available
  • Power supply is available where required (RO systems with UV or smart monitoring)

If an installation cannot proceed due to site conditions that were not disclosed at the time of booking, a call-out fee may apply. We will always contact you prior to booking to confirm suitability where possible.

4 Call-Out Fees

A call-out fee of $280 applies for inspection or quotation visits. This fee is waived in full if the installation proceeds on the same visit or is rescheduled and completed within a reasonable timeframe.

Cancellations within 24 hours of a confirmed booking may incur a cancellation fee to cover time and travel costs.

5 Payment

Full payment is required prior to or on completion of the installation. We accept bank transfer, card payment, and approved finance options where available.

Failure to pay may result in the pursuit of outstanding amounts through debt recovery processes, including any associated costs.

If you sell or vacate the property, the warranty ends for you at that address.

6.2 What makes the warranty valid. All of the following must be true for the Lifetime Warranty to apply and remain in force:

  • The system was purchased as part of a Filters For You installation package and installed by us. Systems bought elsewhere and installed by us, and DIY self-install kits, are not covered by this Lifetime Warranty (see Section 6.11).

  • The system has not been opened, modified or worked on by anyone other than Filters For You (see Section 6.6).

6.3 The first year, and the grace period for the annual service. Every system we install is covered under this Lifetime Warranty from the day we install it until its first yearly service. When that first service is due, the client chooses how we look after the system from then on: the Filtered for Life plan, a professional yearly service, or the DIY kit (the year's cartridges posted to the client). If the client joins the plan, cover continues unbroken. If the client chooses a professional yearly service or the DIY kit, the Lifetime Warranty ends on that due date and Section 6.17 applies from then. A client on a professional yearly service or the DIY kit can join the plan at a later yearly service, and cover under this warranty starts from the day we complete that service. Members have 30 days from the scheduled service due date to complete their annual service. Complete it within that window and cover continues unbroken. If 30 days pass with no service completed, the member is treated as having left the plan and the Lifetime Warranty is voided from the original due date. We remind clients ahead of their due date, but booking and completing the service on time is the client's responsibility.

6.4 What is covered. Every part of the system that we supplied and installed is covered for as long as the client remains eligible under Sections 6.2 and 6.3. That includes:

  • the housings, RO membrane housing, centre joiner, cabinet, manifold and smart internals;
  • every valve, including the pressure limiting valve, inline check valve, shut-off valve, feedwater (inlet) valve, tank valve and leak detection valve;
  • the storage tank;
  • the filtered tap supplied with the system; and
  • pumps, tubing, fittings, connections, O-rings and seals.

If a covered part fails, or wears to the point that it no longer works as it should, we repair or replace it and perform all of the labour (isolating, draining, disconnecting, refitting and pressure testing) at no cost to the client. At each annual service we inspect the system, and any covered part we find to be worn or faulty is replaced at that visit at no charge, or at a return visit at no charge if the part has to be ordered in. There is no call-out fee for a warranty visit.

When we attend. A leak, or a system that has stopped supplying water, gets its own visit, and we attend as soon as we reasonably can. Any other fault in a covered part (for example a valve running water to drain, slow flow or a dripping tap) is fixed at no cost at the next annual service, or at a visit we schedule with the client. The cover is the same either way. Only the timing of the repair differs.

Pure Luxe smart electronics. The Pure Luxe smart electronic components (live monitoring, sensors and display) are covered in full for 5 years from the installation date. After year 5, if the electronics fail, we replace the entire system and perform the installation labour free, and the client pays a fixed contribution of $600.

  • Filter cartridges, RO membranes and other filter media. These are replaced as part of the paid annual service when due, not as a warranty item

  • Damage from causes outside our control, such as water hammer or mains pressure surges, frost, fire, flood, storm, rodent or pest damage, or damage caused by other trades or building works. This affects only the claim for that damage. It does not end the rest of the warranty, and we quote any repair before we carry it out
  • Damage from unauthorised work (see Section 6.6)
  • Water quality issues caused by the property's own plumbing or incoming mains supply, unrelated to the installed system
  • Consequential damage the client chose not to prevent after being notified (see Section 6.8)
  • Mixer taps and other tapware supplied as an upgrade, including the three-way mixer in a Deluxe package. These carry their manufacturer's own tapware warranty, which we confirm with the quote
  • The client's own tapware and plumbing that we did not supply, including the existing kitchen mixer that a Pure Flow system connects to

6.6 Unauthorised tampering or repair voids the entire warranty. If anyone other than Filters For You opens, modifies, services or attempts to repair the system (including the client, a handyman, or another plumber), the Lifetime Warranty is voided in full and permanently, from the date that work occurred, even if it was unrelated to the part that later fails.

If the system is removed, transported or reinstalled anywhere other than by Filters For You at that address, the Lifetime Warranty ends permanently for that unit, even if the Filtered for Life plan is otherwise current. If you need a system relocated (for a renovation, or to move a fridge or sink within the same property), Filters For You must perform the relocation for the warranty to continue. Contact us before any system is disconnected.

6.8 Declining a recommended repair. If we flag an issue at a service and the client declines the recommended repair, the warranty does not extend to any further or consequential damage that results from that declined repair. This affects only the specific consequential claim; it does not cancel the client's broader warranty or Filtered for Life plan standing.

If a member cancels the Filtered for Life plan, moves to a professional yearly service or the DIY kit, or lets the plan lapse beyond the 30 days' grace in Section 6.3, the Lifetime Warranty ends permanently on that system from the date of lapse. There is no reinstatement. Rejoining the plan, paying a back fee or booking a fresh inspection later does not restore Lifetime Warranty cover. A new inspection at that point is a paid service call, not a warranty reinstatement. Section 6.17 continues to apply. The Lifetime Warranty is sustainable only because it is funded by members who stay on the plan, which is why this condition is strict.

6.10 Change of ownership. Because the warranty is non-transferable (Section 6.1), a new owner or tenant at the same address has no claim under the original client's warranty, even if the system is untouched. 6.11 DIY self-install packages. Systems bought as a DIY or self-install kit with no Filters For You installation labour do not carry this Lifetime Warranty. They are covered only by the underlying manufacturer's standard product warranty, where applicable.

For a defect in our own work under Section 6.17, that period is the life of the system at the address where we installed it.
