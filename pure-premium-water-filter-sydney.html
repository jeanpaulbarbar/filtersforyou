Seven stages. Simply explained.

  1. 01

    Sediment

    5 micron prefilter. Catches dirt, rust and sediment before they reach the finer filters.

  2. 02

    Coconut carbon

    5 micron carbon block. Helps reduce chlorine, taste and odour while protecting the RO membrane.

  3. 03

    RO membrane

    50 GPD membrane. Helps reduce dissolved contaminants for purified drinking water.

  4. 04

    Hydrogen & minerals

    T300 mineral stage. Adds magnesium and potassium, raises alkalinity and introduces dissolved hydrogen.

  5. 05

    Ceramic media

    An inline ceramic media stage, fitted after the mineral cartridge in the seven stage system.

  6. 06

    Final carbon polish

    Activated coconut carbon helps reduce remaining taste and odour before the drinking tap.

  7. 07

    Inline conditioner

    A compact magnetic inline component completing the seven stage configuration.

No. It supplies purified water through your kitchen drinking tap. Whole house filtration is a separate system.
