What is humm?

humm offers payment plans that let you buy now and pay over time. Plans are provided by Once Credit Pty Limited, a member of humm group, Australian Credit Licence No. 386194. Filters For You offers finance through humm on every system we install.

Can I pay off a water filter over time?

Yes. Choose your system, pick a humm term of 6, 12, 18 or 24 months and pay it off with fortnightly repayments. Interest free applies to purchases up to $10,000. Payment plans are for approved applicants only.

Is it interest free?

Yes, on purchases up to $10,000. humm charges no interest on its 6, 12, 18 or 24 month plans up to that amount. humm’s own fees still apply: an $80 establishment fee and a $9.95 monthly fee. Our price with humm also carries humm’s merchant fee, the cost of having the plan available to you. Every fee shows in the bill before you ask for a plan.

How much is it a fortnight?

It depends on the system and the term. Pure Home, installed from $3,850, comes to $84 a fortnight over 24 months, plus humm’s $9.95 monthly fee, a total amount payable of $4,572.94. The repayments calculator shows every system and every term side by side.

What fees does humm charge?

humm charges an $80 establishment fee, added to the amount financed and spread across the term, and a $9.95 monthly fee that humm bills separately to your fortnightly repayment. Then there is humm’s merchant fee, the cost of having the plan available to you. We build it into your price, which is how your plan stays interest free on purchases up to $10,000.

Which systems can I pay off with humm?

Every system we install. At its listed price, each one comes in under humm’s $10,000 interest free limit on at least one term. Prices include installation and GST.

  • Pure Home, from $3,850 installed
  • Pure Home Reserve, from $5,150 installed
  • Pure Flow, $810 installed
  • Pure Essential, $1,180 installed
  • Pure Plus, $1,280 installed
  • Pure Premium, $1,590 installed
  • Pure Luxe, $2,290 installed
  • Deluxe Home Package, from $6,290 installed
  • Deluxe Home Reserve, from $8,640 installed

Who can apply for humm?

humm decides who is approved. In humm’s own words, before you apply you need to:

  • Be 18+ years old
  • Permanently reside in Australia
  • Hold a valid Australian ID
  • Have a regular source of income

Payment plans are for approved applicants only. humm’s full terms are on hummloan.com.

How do I apply?

Pick your system in the calculator and tap Request this payment plan. We call you and confirm your price in writing, then send you humm’s link. You apply with humm directly, online or in the humm app, and humm decides on your application. We never fill in the application for you.

Which term should I choose?

That is your call. The calculator shows every term side by side, each with its fortnightly repayment and its total amount payable, and none is chosen for you. A term that is not available at your price says why.

Does paying with humm change my warranty?

No. Your system carries the same lifetime warranty however you pay. It is covered for life while you stay serviced with us. Read the warranty terms.
