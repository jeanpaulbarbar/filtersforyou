How it works

No new holes. Your mixer goes back when you move.

What happens at your sink, from the day it's fitted to the day you move out.

  1. 1

    Today: your mixer, your tap hole.

    Your kitchen mixer sits in its tap hole on the hot and cold water. That's where we start, and it's where we finish.

  2. 2

    Your mixer comes off and is kept aside.

    A licensed plumber takes your mixer off and puts it aside, ready for moving day. No new holes are drilled.

  3. 3

    A three way mixer goes in the same hole.

    A brand new three way mixer fits the tap hole you already have and connects to the existing plumbing. Reverse osmosis sits in the cupboard under the sink.

  4. 4

    Filtered water, from the same tap.

    Hot and cold work as they always have. Purified water has its own control and its own line to the tap. No more bottles coming through the front door.

  5. 5

    Moving day: your mixer goes back.

    The three way mixer comes out of the same tap hole. Your original mixer goes back on and is reconnected, as it was.

Clean water isn't only for owners

Sydney's water is safe, but it is not pure. Most of Sydney is treated with chloramine, and you can't boil it off or leave it sitting. Renting doesn't change what's in the water. It changes who you ask before any plumbing work.

Talk to your landlord first

Every lease is different, so ask your landlord or agent first, in writing, and keep their reply. Say what you'd like fitted, that a licensed plumber fits it, that no new holes are drilled, and that the original mixer goes back on when you move. Our ready message above says all of that.

We can't give legal advice on your lease. If you're unsure, check your lease or ask NSW Fair Trading.

Under sink systems for Sydney renters

Reverse osmosis, Pure Essential, Pure Premium or Pure Luxe, sits under the sink in the kitchen cupboard. Each comes with a chrome filtered tap, which needs its own hole. For renters, a three way mixer takes its place in the tap hole you already have, so no new holes are drilled.

Hot and cold work as they always have, and purified water has its own control and its own line to the tap. The Classic three way mixer is $480 with your system, so Pure Essential and the mixer are $1,570 together, installed. See every mixer in Mixer Tap Studio.

Taking your water filter when you move

When you move out, the three way mixer comes out of the same tap hole. Your original mixer goes back on and is reconnected, as it was.

Whole house filtration and rentals

Whole house filtration goes in where water enters the property, so it's a job for the owner to approve. If your landlord is interested, Pure Home is from $2,950. We can send them the details.

Can I install a water filter in a rental?

Often, with your landlord's okay. Ask first, in writing, and keep their reply. For renters we drill no new holes: your mixer comes off and is kept aside, and a three way mixer goes in the same tap hole.

Do I need my landlord's permission?

Ask first. Every lease is different, and a water filter means plumbing work under the sink. We can't give legal advice on your lease, so if you're unsure, check your lease or ask NSW Fair Trading.

Can I take my water filter when I move out?

It can come out. When you move out, the three way mixer comes out of the same tap hole, and your original mixer goes back on and is reconnected, as it was.

Does a filtered tap need a hole?

A filtered tap on its own needs its own hole in the sink or benchtop. For renters we fit a three way mixer instead, in the tap hole you already have, so no new holes are drilled. Hot, cold and filtered water come from one tap.

What does a renter's setup cost?

Pure Essential is $1,090 installed and the Classic three way mixer is $480, so $1,570 together. With Pure Premium it's $1,970, and with Pure Luxe $2,470. Every price includes supply, installation by a licensed plumber and GST.

How much does a water filter cost in Sydney?

Reverse osmosis starts at $1,090 for Pure Essential, then $1,490 for Pure Premium and $1,990 for Pure Luxe. Whole house filtration starts at $2,950 for Pure Home and $4,150 for Pure Home Reserve. The Deluxe setups are from $4,040 and $5,640. Every price includes supply, installation by a licensed plumber and GST.

What does a water filter cost each year?

You choose at your first service. A DIY kit is $140 to $390 a year: we post the cartridges and you change them. A professional yearly service is $310 to $550: we change every cartridge and check the system. Filtered for Life is $380 to $700 and adds the lifetime warranty. It depends on the system.
