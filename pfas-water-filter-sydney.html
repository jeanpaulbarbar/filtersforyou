The short version

What Are PFAS and Why Do They Matter?

PFAS is short for per and polyfluoroalkyl substances, a family of man made chemicals. The Australian guidelines set values for PFOS, PFOA, PFHxS and PFBS, and Sydney Water reports all four.

They matter to a lot of families because they’d rather not have them in the glass at all, guideline or not. That’s a fair thing to want.

What helps

Does a Standard Water Filter Remove PFAS?

For PFAS in the water you drink, reverse osmosis is the system.

The membrane works down to about 0.0001 micron, and helps remove PFAS, microplastics, dissolved metals and salts. Reverse osmosis helps remove up to 99% of dissolved contaminants, as published by the manufacturer.

Our whole house systems help reduce chlorine and chloramine at every tap. For PFAS in what you drink, add reverse osmosis at the kitchen sink.
