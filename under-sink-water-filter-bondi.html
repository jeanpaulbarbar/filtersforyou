Under Sink Water Filters for Bondi Apartments

Bondi has one of Sydney's highest concentrations of apartments and units, from the iconic high-rises facing Bondi Beach to the strata complexes throughout North Bondi, Bondi Junction and the streets between. The vast majority of Bondi residents live in apartments, which means whole house filtration systems that connect to the mains are simply not a practical option for most people.

This is exactly what under sink water filters are designed for. The system connects directly to the cold water supply line under your kitchen sink, the same pipe that already supplies your tap. No building mains access required. No need to involve strata or body corporate in most cases. We fit the system under your sink, install a small dedicated filtered tap through your benchtop (typically next to your existing taps), and the job is done in under two hours.

Bondi renters can often have under sink filters installed too, the system uses standard plumbing connections and can be removed and reinstalled if you move. If you're renting, we can talk you through what's involved for your specific situation.

Why Bondi Residents Choose Filtered Water

Sydney's tap water meets health safety standards, but it's treated with chlorine and chloramines that leave a distinct taste and smell many people find unpleasant. For Bondi residents who are health-conscious, active and switched-on about what they're putting into their bodies, an under sink filter is a natural choice.

A carbon filter helps reduce chlorine taste and odour. Reverse osmosis goes further: Pure Essential takes up to 99% of dissolved contaminants out through a 0.0001 micron membrane, then puts calcium back in. That's purified water on tap. For chloramine, the answer is a whole house system at the mains, because the membrane doesn't take it out. Many Bondi households that previously bought bottled water switch to RO and never look back, better water at a fraction of the ongoing cost.

How the Installation Works

We arrive with all parts and fittings needed for your specific property. The process is straightforward:

  • Cold water supply line under the sink is connected to the system
  • Filter housing is mounted inside the cabinet, compact and unobtrusive
  • A small hole (typically 12–14mm) is drilled through the benchtop for the dedicated filtered drinking tap
  • System is flushed, tested and commissioned
  • We walk you through operation and cartridge replacement schedules

The entire process takes approximately 1.5 to 2 hours. We leave your kitchen clean and tidy, and you have filtered drinking water flowing before we go.

Pure Essential vs Pure Premium: Which Is Right for You?

Both are reverse osmosis systems with a 0.0001 micron membrane and both deliver significantly better water than straight tap water. The main difference is what goes back into the water after the membrane.

The Pure Essential ($1,090) is 4 Stage Neutralising Reverse Osmosis. Up to 99% of dissolved contaminants come out, then calcium goes back in for a neutral, gentle finish at about pH 7 to 8. If your priority is purified drinking water and an end to bottled water, the Essential does this very well.

The Pure Premium ($1,490) is 7 Stage Hydrogen Rich Alkaline Reverse Osmosis, our highest specification and our bestseller. After the membrane, a mineral stage adds magnesium and potassium and introduces dissolved hydrogen. That's alkaline water on tap, every day, for the family.

Not sure which is right for you? Call Jean-Paul on 0430 546 749 for an honest recommendation based on your water use and priorities.

Bondi Water Quality

Sydney Water supplies Bondi from the Prospect and Woronora systems via an extensive distribution network. By the time water reaches a Bondi tap, particularly in older apartment buildings, it has travelled through pipes that may introduce additional sediment, copper from copper plumbing, and residual chlorine. The distinctive Bondi Beach area also has a high proportion of older building stock where pipe condition can affect water quality at the point of use.

An under sink filter addresses quality at the final point, your drinking tap, regardless of pipe condition throughout the building. This is why it's the most practical and cost-effective solution for Bondi apartment residents.
