What is in Concord’s water?

Sydney Water supplies treated drinking water to Concord. Chlorine and chloramine help maintain that water through the distribution network, and can influence how it tastes or smells. Sydney water is safe, but not pure. Selecting filtration is a choice about drinking water or wider household coverage. Internal plumbing differs between properties and renovations, so the suburb’s history or a home’s appearance does not establish what is happening in its pipes.
