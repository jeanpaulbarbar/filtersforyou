Why Parramatta Homeowners Choose Pure Home

Where an under sink filter treats only the drinking water at one tap, Pure Home treats all the water entering your home. Filtered water from every outlet, a shower that is kinder to skin and hair, less chlorine smell in the bathroom, filtered water for laundry and cooking, and less sediment reaching appliances and the hot water system. It is complete home filtration, and its cost is published: from $2,950 installed.

Disinfectants can be harsh on skin, especially in a hot shower, and Pure Home helps reduce that. Chlorine and chloramine can be hard on hair over time, the same reason swimmers get that dry, brassy look. Chlorine gasses off. Chloramine doesn’t. It is built to last the whole way through the pipes, so it is still there in the shower, the laundry and the dishwasher, and Pure Home helps reduce it at the mains.

The sediment stage also catches silt, dirt and rust before they reach your hot water system, dishwasher and washing machine, which helps protect your appliances and plumbing.

What Pure Home Filters from Parramatta Water

Pure Home works in three stages at your Parramatta home’s mains entry:

  • Sediment first. A grooved sediment cartridge catches silt, dirt and rust. Dirt, rust and grit travel down the street main whenever the network is worked on.
  • Chlorine and chloramine. A coconut carbon block helps reduce both, the source of the taste and smell of Sydney tap water, before any shower, tap or appliance.
  • Down to 0.22 micron. The finest carbon stage, before every tap and shower.
  • Calcium and magnesium stay in. The good minerals carry on to every tap. This is a chemical reduction system built for Sydney, not a softener.

For purified drinking water as well, add reverse osmosis under the kitchen sink. Is a Whole House Filter or Under-Sink RO Right for Your Parramatta Property?

It depends on your property type. Whole house filtration is for Parramatta houses, townhouses and properties with access to the mains supply line. It covers all the water in the home. Under sink reverse osmosis is for apartments, units and any property without mains access, or as a drinking water upgrade for homeowners who already have whole house filtration.

For Parramatta apartments, Pure Essential, 4 stage neutralising reverse osmosis at $1,090, is the best starting point. For Parramatta homeowners, Pure Home from $2,950 filters every tap, shower and bath. Auburn and the suburbs around Parramatta are covered the same way.

What is a whole house water filter and how does it work in Parramatta?

A whole house water filter connects at your Parramatta home’s main water supply line and filters all the water entering the property: every tap, shower, bath and appliance. Is a whole house filter or under sink RO better for my Parramatta property?

Whole house filtration suits Parramatta houses and townhouses with mains access, and filters all the water in the home. Under sink reverse osmosis is best for apartments without mains access, or as a purified drinking water upgrade. Many homeowners choose both, and Deluxe Home packages Pure Home with Pure Essential from $4,040.

Can a whole house water filter be installed in a Parramatta apartment?

Whole house systems need access to the property’s main water supply line and suit houses and townhouses. For Parramatta apartments without mains access, we recommend reverse osmosis under the kitchen sink instead, from $1,090. Call 0430 546 749 to talk about your property.

Does a whole house water filter remove chloramine?

Both of ours help reduce it, and in Sydney that is the job that matters most. The coconut carbon block and the finest carbon stage are where it happens, with the reduction as published by the manufacturer.

Do whole house water filters reduce water pressure?

On most homes you will not notice a difference at the tap. Both systems run three full size cartridges, and we check the system at every yearly service.

Does a whole house water filter need power or a drain?

Neither of ours does. They run on your mains pressure, so there is no power point, no drain line and no waste water.
