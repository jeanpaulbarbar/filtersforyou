Under sink vs benchtop, which water filter is right for you?

It comes down to your bench, your plumbing and how long you’ll be in the home.

A benchtop filter needs no installation, so it suits a short stay or a home where you can’t change the plumbing. An under sink system is the permanent choice. It sits in the cupboard, gives you your own filtered tap and keeps the bench clear.

Benchtop water filters, what they are and where they work

A filter that sits on the bench.

Benchtop filters come in three main kinds. Gravity fed units you pour water into, and it filters down through a cartridge into a lower tank. Direct connect units attach to your kitchen tap with a diverter you switch when you want filtered water. And countertop reverse osmosis units, which are bulkier and still sit on the bench.

Their strengths are real: no installation, no plumber, no landlord permission and no tap hole, and they move house with you. We don’t install benchtop filters.

Under sink water filtration systems, how ours work

Reverse osmosis in the cupboard, purified water at its own tap.

A 0.0001 micron membrane takes up to 99% of dissolved contaminants out, then minerals go back in. Pure Essential is 4 stage neutralising reverse osmosis, Pure Premium is 7 Stage Hydrogen Rich Alkaline Reverse Osmosis and Pure Luxe is smart 5 stage alkaline reverse osmosis with a live purity reading. Installed from $1,090, by a licensed plumber.

Water comes straight from its own tap, so there’s nothing to pour in and wait for. On the bench, the only sign of it is the filtered tap.

Looking after an under sink water filter

Fresh cartridges, once a year.

Every cartridge is changed once a year, and the reverse osmosis membrane when it’s due. On a DIY kit we post the year’s cartridges and you change them. On a Professional yearly service or Filtered for Life, we come out, change them and check the system while we’re there. Covered for life while you stay on the Filtered for Life plan.

What is the difference between benchtop and under sink water filters?

A benchtop filter sits on your bench and needs no installation. An under sink system sits in the cupboard under the sink and gives you your own filtered tap, so the bench stays clear. Ours are reverse osmosis, with a 0.0001 micron membrane.

Do I need a plumber for an under sink filter?

Ours are installed by a licensed plumber, NSW licence 461511C, and the installation is included in the price.

Which is better for a rental, benchtop or under sink?

A benchtop filter needs no installation, so it suits a short stay or a home where you can’t change the plumbing. An under sink system needs a hole in the sink or bench for its tap, so in a rental, ask your landlord first.

How much is an under sink water filter in Sydney?

Pure Essential is $1,090, Pure Premium $1,490 and Pure Luxe $1,990, including GST, supply and installation. Each year after that you choose a DIY kit, a Professional yearly service or the Filtered for Life plan.

Can I get cold filtered water from an under sink system?

Yes. Add the undersink chiller to Pure Essential, Pure Premium or Pure Luxe, $1,050 installed with your system. It chills your filtered water to around 10 degrees, with about eight glasses ready to pour.

How long does an under sink install take?

About 1.5 to 2 hours for an under sink reverse osmosis system, installed and tested by a licensed plumber.
