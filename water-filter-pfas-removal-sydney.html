Filter by filter

Which Filter Types Remove PFAS?

  • Reverse osmosis

    The system for PFAS in the water you drink. Reverse osmosis helps remove up to 99% of dissolved contaminants, as published by the manufacturer.

  • Whole house systems

    Help reduce chlorine and chloramine at every tap. For PFAS in what you drink, add reverse osmosis.

  • Carbon at the tap

    Pure Flow helps reduce chlorine taste on your own mixer. We don’t sell it for PFAS.

Inside the system

How Reverse Osmosis Works

Water is pushed under pressure through a membrane with openings around 0.0001 micron. PFAS, microplastics, dissolved metals and salts are rejected and flushed to the drain.

Carbon stages help reduce the chlorine, and the taste and smell go with it. The membrane doesn’t take chloramine out. The carbon does.

What is the best filter to remove PFAS?

Reverse osmosis, for the water you drink. The membrane works down to about 0.0001 micron, and helps remove PFAS, microplastics, dissolved metals and salts.

How much does a PFAS removal filter cost in Sydney?

Pure Essential is $1,090 installed, Pure Premium $1,490 and Pure Luxe $1,990, all including GST, supply and installation. Each year after, it’s the DIY kit, the professional yearly service or Filtered for Life. The table on this page has every price.

Is there PFAS in Sydney water?

Traces. Sydney Water tests the treated water at each plant, and over the year every result met the guideline. At Prospect, PFOS peaked at 0.0019 µg/L against a guideline of 0.008.

Does a carbon filter remove PFAS?

We don’t sell our carbon systems for PFAS. They’re built for chlorine and chloramine. Reverse osmosis is the system for PFAS in what you drink.

Does reverse osmosis take the minerals out?

It purifies, then our range puts good minerals back: calcium in Pure Essential and Pure Luxe, magnesium and potassium in Pure Premium.
