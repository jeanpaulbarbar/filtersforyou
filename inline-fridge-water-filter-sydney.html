What an inline fridge water filter does

A small filter on the water line that feeds your fridge.

A fridge or refrigerator with a water dispenser or an ice maker is connected to a water line, usually run from under the kitchen sink or behind the fridge. An inline fridge filter is a cartridge fitted on that line, so the water is filtered before it reaches the dispenser and the ice. What it takes out, and how often it is changed, depends on the cartridge, so follow the maker’s guide for yours.

We don’t sell or install inline fridge filters. Below is what we install instead, for the water you drink every day.

Cold filtered water at the kitchen sink

Reverse osmosis with the undersink chiller.

If cold water is the reason you want a fridge filter, the undersink chiller gives you that at your kitchen sink. It goes in with Pure Essential, Pure Premium or Pure Luxe and chills your purified water on its way to the tap, to around 10 degrees, with about eight glasses ready to pour from a stainless steel tank.

The chiller is $1,050 installed with your system, so Pure Essential with a chiller is $2,140, Pure Premium $2,540 and Pure Luxe $3,040. It needs a power point under the sink, carries the maker’s 3 year warranty, parts and labour, and needs no yearly servicing. It is not part of the lifetime warranty on the Filtered for Life plan. More on the undersink chiller.

Purified water to drink and cook with

Under the sink, at your kitchen tap.

Our reverse osmosis systems use a 0.0001 micron membrane that helps remove up to 99% of dissolved contaminants, then minerals go back in. Pure Essential is 4 stage neutralising reverse osmosis at $1,090. Pure Premium is 7 Stage Hydrogen Rich Alkaline Reverse Osmosis at $1,490, our bestseller. Pure Luxe is smart 5 stage alkaline reverse osmosis with a live purity reading at $1,990. Each one comes with its own filtered tap. See every under sink water filter we install.

Want to keep your kitchen mixer? Pure Flow filters the cold water to the mixer you already have, with a 0.5 micron carbon block that helps reduce chlorine taste and odour. No new tap and no tank, $760 installed.

Filtered water at every tap, from the mains

Pure Home filters the water where it enters your home.

Pure Home is 3 stage whole house filtration, filtered down to 0.22 micron. Every tap, shower and appliance gets filtered water, it helps reduce chlorine and chloramine, and calcium and magnesium stay in. Pure Home is from $2,950 installed. Pure Home Reserve puts the same filtration inside a lockable stainless cabinet in 22 colours, from $4,150.

The questions

Fridge filter questions.

Short, honest answers.

What is an inline fridge water filter?

A small filter cartridge fitted on the water line that feeds a fridge or refrigerator with a water dispenser or an ice maker. The water is filtered before it reaches the dispenser and the ice. We don’t sell or install inline fridge filters.

Do inline fridge water filters need a licensed plumber?

We don’t install inline fridge filters, so check with your fridge maker or a licensed plumber about the one you have in mind. Everything we install is fitted by a licensed plumber, NSW licence 461511C.

How much does inline fridge filter installation cost in Sydney?

We don’t sell or install inline fridge filters, so we don’t price them. For cold filtered water at your kitchen sink, the undersink chiller is $1,050 installed with Pure Essential, Pure Premium or Pure Luxe. Pure Premium with a chiller is $2,540 installed.

What does an inline fridge filter remove?

That depends on the cartridge, so check the maker’s details for the one you’re looking at. Our reverse osmosis systems use a 0.0001 micron membrane that helps remove up to 99% of dissolved contaminants, then minerals go back in.

How often does an inline fridge filter need replacing?

Follow the fridge or filter maker’s guide for yours. With our systems you choose each year: a DIY kit, where we post the year’s cartridges, a Professional yearly service, where we come out and change every cartridge, or the Filtered for Life plan, which adds the lifetime warranty.

Can I get cold filtered water without a fridge filter?

Yes. The undersink chiller goes in with Pure Essential, Pure Premium or Pure Luxe and chills your filtered water on its way to the tap, to around 10 degrees, with about eight glasses ready to pour. It is $1,050 installed with your system and needs a power point under the sink.
