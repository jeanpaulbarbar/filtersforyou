What an RO System Removes from Chatswood Tap Water

Chatswood is on Sydney Water’s treated supply, which meets the Australian Drinking Water Guidelines. Chlorine and chloramine keep it safe through the network, and you may notice either in the taste or smell. Sydney’s water is safe, but it is not pure. Sydney Water publishes what is in the supply.

Our reverse osmosis systems help remove up to 99% of dissolved contaminants. The membrane works down to about 0.0001 micron, which helps reduce heavy metals such as lead and copper, nitrates, PFAS and dissolved salts, and helps remove microplastics. The membrane’s maker publishes tested reduction rates for its element, compliant to NSF/ANSI 58: lead 98%, arsenic 98%, chlorine 98% and up, trihalomethanes 98% and up, sodium 95%, nitrate 90%.

Chloramine is the exception. The membrane doesn’t take it out. The carbon does, so for chloramine at every tap and shower the answer is a whole house system at the mains.

How Reverse Osmosis Works

Mains water comes into the cabinet and passes through the stages in order. Four stage reverse osmosis, that’s purified water on tap.

  • Sediment. A five micron spun cartridge takes the grit, rust and sand out first.
  • Carbon block. Coconut carbon helps reduce the chlorine, and with it the taste and the smell.
  • The membrane. Water is pushed under pressure through a membrane with openings around 0.0001 micron. PFAS, microplastics, dissolved metals and salts are rejected and flushed to the drain.
  • Calcium and polish. Calcium goes back in for a gentler pH, then one last pass through coconut carbon before the tap. We remineralise, because minerals are good.

That is Pure Essential. Pure Premium adds stages after the membrane, a hydrogen rich alkaline stage, a ceramic stage and an inline conditioner, which is where its seven come from. Pure Luxe runs five stages in quick change cartridges inside a compact sealed case, with a display that shows the purity reading live. The purified water is stored in a small pressurised tank under the sink and delivered through a dedicated filtered tap. The whole system fits inside a standard kitchen cabinet.

What does a reverse osmosis system remove from Chatswood tap water?

A reverse osmosis system helps remove up to 99% of dissolved contaminants, including heavy metals such as lead, nitrates, PFAS and microplastics, as published by the manufacturer. Does reverse osmosis remove the good minerals too?

The membrane does the deep separation first, then the system puts minerals back. Pure Essential adds calcium for a neutral finish, Pure Premium adds hydrogen richness with magnesium and potassium, and Pure Luxe adds calcium and shows the purity reading live. Minerals are good water.
