Want the detail on filtration? +

Different filters target different substances. Carbon helps reduce chlorine and chloramine; an RO membrane adds treatment for dissolved substances such as fluoride. Performance depends on the system, water conditions and cartridge care.

Microplastics are particles; fluoride and phosphates are dissolved substances. Those are different filtration questions. An exact reduction percentage needs testing of the particular system for the particular substance.

The supplied laboratory report B767408 compares Gold Coast tap water with RO and alkaline outputs in 2019. It illustrates treatment under those test conditions, rather than proving a removal percentage for every FFY installation.

Pure Premium’s alkaline and hydrogen features are supported by the supplier’s cartridge data. These describe the water, without promising health outcomes. For a plain explanation of the technologies, see the CDC’s home water treatment guide.

What’s the difference between whole house and reverse osmosis?

Whole-house filtration treats the incoming water for your household outlets. Reverse osmosis adds a finer purification step for your drinking water.

Pure Home uses sediment filtration and catalytic carbon to help reduce sediment, chlorine and chloramine. Showers, baths, laundry and the kitchen all receive treated water. Under-sink RO uses a membrane to reduce dissolved substances such as fluoride at a dedicated drinking tap. Coverage and treatment depth are different questions.

Which is better for your home?

Start with where you want to notice the change.

Choose whole house when your priority is water across the household. Choose RO when your priority is extra purification for drinking and cooking. Neither makes the other pointless. On a drinkable mains supply, whole-house filtration is a useful improvement at the kitchen tap as well as everywhere else. Explore Pure Home or compare the RO systems for the detailed specifications.

Can you use whole house and reverse osmosis together?

Yes. They do different jobs, in the same home.

The whole-house system treats water as it enters. The RO system then adds membrane purification at the kitchen. The Deluxe Home Package brings Pure Home, Pure Premium seven-stage hydrogen-rich alkaline RO and a premium three-way mixer together, from $6,290 installed including GST. Your final quote depends on the installation. You can also start with one system and discuss the second later.

Where do alkaline and hydrogen-rich water fit?

They describe the finish of the RO drinking water.

The membrane performs the separation first; the finishing stages then remineralise the water. Pure Plus is the alkaline starting point. Pure Premium adds a hydrogen-rich alkaline stage. Pure Luxe is the smart alkaline option. Whole-house filtration retains naturally occurring dissolved minerals. These features describe treatment and water characteristics; they do not establish health benefits. See the individual RO options.

What about fluoride, microplastics and reduction percentages?

Different substances need different treatment and test evidence.

An RO membrane adds treatment for dissolved substances such as fluoride. Microplastics are particles, while fluoride and phosphates are dissolved substances. A blanket reduction percentage across all of them needs testing for each substance on the particular system. The supplied laboratory report records specific RO and alkaline outputs in 2019; it does not prove a reduction percentage for every FFY system. Pure Premium’s finishing features are described in the supplier’s cartridge data.

What’s the difference between whole house and reverse osmosis?

Whole-house filtration treats incoming water before it reaches your household taps, showers and baths. An under-sink reverse osmosis system adds membrane purification at a dedicated drinking tap. They cover different needs and can work together.

Which is better, whole house or reverse osmosis?

Whole house is the better fit if your priority is filtered water throughout your home. RO is the better fit if your priority is extra purification for drinking and cooking. The Deluxe Home Package combines whole-house filtration, Pure Premium RO and a premium three-way mixer for both needs.

Can I drink the water with just a whole-house filter?

Yes. On a safe, drinkable mains supply, whole-house filtration also treats the water at your kitchen tap. It helps reduce sediment, chlorine and chloramine throughout your home. RO is an optional additional step for drinking water, rather than something every whole-house customer must add.

Do I need to install both at the same time?

No. You can choose the system that meets your priority now and discuss adding the other later. If you already want both, the Deluxe Home Package brings the whole-house system, Pure Premium RO and a premium three-way mixer into one planned installation.
