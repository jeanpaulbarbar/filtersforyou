Which system fits your home?

Whole house filtration for every tap. Reverse osmosis for the water you drink. Deluxe for both.

Whole house filtration is installed at the mains, so every tap, shower and appliance gets filtered water. Pure Home uses three full size stages with catalytic carbon. Pure Home Reserve uses coconut carbon and a 0.22 micron stage in a cabinet in 22 colours.

Reverse osmosis sits under the kitchen sink with its own filtered tap. Pure Essential, Pure Plus, Pure Premium and Pure Luxe help remove up to 99% of dissolved contaminants, then put minerals back. Pure Flow is not reverse osmosis. It filters the cold water to the mixer you already have.

Want to know what is in your water first? Book a free water assessment.

The Deluxe Home Package and Deluxe Home Reserve put whole house filtration, reverse osmosis and a three way mixer together. Compare whole house and reverse osmosis.

What is the difference between Pure Plus, Premium and Luxe?

Pure Plus is five stage alkaline reverse osmosis. Pure Premium is seven stage hydrogen rich alkaline reverse osmosis, with magnesium, potassium and hydrogen back in. Pure Luxe is smart five stage alkaline reverse osmosis, with live TDS readings on its display and quick change cartridges. Pure Essential is four stage reverse osmosis with a neutral finish.

What makes Pure Home Reserve different?

Pure Home Reserve is whole house filtration in a powder coated stainless steel cabinet, in 22 colours matched to the COLORBOND® palette. Inside are grooved sediment, coconut carbon and a 0.22 micron multi stage carbon cartridge, which help reduce chlorine and chloramine at every tap. It starts at $5,150 installed including GST. See Pure Home Reserve.

Can I combine whole house filtration and reverse osmosis?

Yes. The Deluxe Home Package includes Pure Home, Pure Premium reverse osmosis and a premium three way mixer, from $6,290 installed including GST. The Deluxe Home Reserve includes Pure Home Reserve, Pure Luxe and a three way mixer, from $8,640. Both include the first year of servicing free.

Do I need a separate filtered tap?

Pure Essential, Pure Plus, Pure Premium and Pure Luxe include a chrome filtered tap. You can choose a three way mixer instead, which delivers hot, cold and purified water through one fixture, with a separate filtered water path. See every tap and mixer.

Does the lifetime warranty cover everything?

It covers the sealed system and the labour to replace a covered part while your paid annual service plan remains active. Valves, tapware and smart electronics have separate limits. Cartridges and routine replacement parts are separate. Read the cover and conditions.

Is Pure Flow a reverse osmosis system?

No. Pure Flow is three in one inline filtration on the cold water line to the kitchen mixer you already have. It helps reduce chlorine taste and odour, and no new tap is needed. For purified water, the reverse osmosis range starts with Pure Essential at $1,180 installed.

Is Sydney tap water safe to drink?

Yes. Sydney’s water is safe, but it is not pure. Sydney Water treats it with chlorine or chloramine so it stays safe all the way to your tap. Whole house filtration helps take that back out at your home, and reverse osmosis purifies the water you drink. Is Sydney tap water safe?

What does a yearly service include?

We book the visit with you, replace every scheduled cartridge, and check and test the whole system. The same local team that installed it comes back each year, and each service keeps your lifetime cover active.
