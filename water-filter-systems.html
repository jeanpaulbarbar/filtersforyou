Which system fits your home?

Whole house filtration for every tap. Reverse osmosis for the water you drink. Deluxe for both.

Whole house filtration is installed at the mains, so every tap, shower and appliance gets filtered water. Pure Home is three stage filtration on a wall bracket: sediment first, then two carbon stages, down to 0.22 micron. Pure Home Reserve is the same filtration inside a lockable stainless cabinet in 22 colours.

Reverse osmosis sits under the kitchen sink with its own filtered tap. Pure Essential, Pure Premium and Pure Luxe help remove up to 99% of dissolved contaminants, as published by the manufacturer, then put minerals back. Pure Flow is not reverse osmosis. It filters the cold water to the mixer you already have.

Want to know what is in your water first? Book a free water assessment.

Deluxe Home and Deluxe Home Reserve put whole house filtration, reverse osmosis and the Classic three way mixer together, with the mixer free. Compare whole house and reverse osmosis.

How does lifetime cover work?

Covered for life while you stay on the Filtered for Life plan.

Every part of the system we supply and install is covered for life, with the labour and the callout, while you stay on the plan. A leak or no water gets its own visit. Any other covered fault is fixed at your next service or a booked visit. New systems are covered from install day until the first yearly service, where you choose. You can join the plan at a later service. Leaving it, or missing a service by more than 30 days, ends the lifetime warranty for good. Without the plan, you have the maker’s parts warranty and our lifetime promise on our own work. Read the full warranty.

What is the difference between Pure Essential, Premium and Luxe?

All three are reverse osmosis. Pure Essential is four stage neutralising reverse osmosis, with calcium back in for about pH 7 to 8. Pure Premium is seven stage hydrogen rich alkaline reverse osmosis, with magnesium, potassium and hydrogen back in. Pure Luxe is smart five stage alkaline reverse osmosis, with a live purity display and quick change cartridges.

What is the difference between Pure Home and Pure Home Reserve?

Both are three stage whole house filtration: sediment first, then two carbon stages, down to 0.22 micron. They help reduce chlorine and chloramine at every tap and shower. Pure Home is three black housings on a wall bracket, from $2,950 installed. Pure Home Reserve puts the same filtration in a lockable stainless cabinet in 22 colours, from $4,150 installed. See Pure Home Reserve.

Can I combine whole house filtration and reverse osmosis?

Yes. Deluxe Home is Pure Home and Pure Essential with the Classic three way mixer free, from $4,040 installed. Deluxe Home Reserve is Pure Home Reserve and Pure Premium with the Classic mixer free and the first year of servicing free on both, from $5,640. GST included.

Do I need a separate filtered tap?

Pure Essential, Pure Premium and Pure Luxe include a filtered tap. You can choose a three way mixer instead, with hot, cold and filtered water from one tap. The Classic three way mixer is $480 on its own, and free in both Deluxe setups. See every tap and mixer.

Does the lifetime warranty cover everything?

It comes with the Filtered for Life plan. Every part of the system we supply and install is covered for life, with the labour and the callout, while you stay on the plan. Cartridges are part of the yearly service. Pure Luxe electronics have five years in full, and mixer taps carry their maker’s tapware warranty. Read the cover and conditions.

What is Filtered for Free?

For every friend or family member who installs Pure Essential or above, your next year of cartridges is free. It is for every customer, member or not, with no limit. On a DIY kit, that year’s kit is posted free. With a professional yearly service or Filtered for Life, you pay $190 for the visit only.

Is Pure Flow a reverse osmosis system?

No. Pure Flow is three in one inline filtration on the cold water line to the kitchen mixer you already have. It helps reduce chlorine taste and odour, and no new tap is needed. For purified water, reverse osmosis starts with Pure Essential at $1,090 installed.

Can I have chilled filtered water?

Yes. The undersink chiller is an add on at $1,050 installed with a system. It chills your filtered water to around 10 degrees, with about eight glasses ready to pour. It needs a power point under the sink and has no yearly servicing. It carries the maker’s 3 year warranty, parts and labour, and is not part of the lifetime warranty.

Is Sydney tap water safe to drink?

Yes. Sydney’s water is safe, but it is not pure. Sydney Water treats it with chlorine or chloramine so it stays safe all the way to your tap. Whole house filtration helps take that back out at your home, and reverse osmosis purifies the water you drink. Is Sydney tap water safe?

Who installs and looks after the systems?

Filters For You supplies, installs and services every system across Sydney. A licensed plumber installs it, NSW licence 461511C. Whole house filtration covers every tap and shower from $2,950 installed, reverse osmosis for your drinking water starts at $1,090 with Pure Essential, and the Deluxe setups do both, from $4,040, with the Classic three way mixer free. Every system is covered for life while you stay on the Filtered for Life plan. Call 0430 546 749.

What does a yearly service include?

We book the visit with you, replace every scheduled cartridge, and check and test the whole system. On the Filtered for Life plan, each service also keeps your lifetime warranty going.
