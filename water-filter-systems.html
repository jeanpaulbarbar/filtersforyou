Which system fits your home?

Reverse osmosis for drinking water. Whole house filtration for every tap.

Pure Flow filters the cold water line of your existing mixer. Pure Essential, Pure Plus, Pure Premium and Pure Luxe are under sink reverse osmosis options. Pure Home and Pure Home Reserve treat the incoming water for your home. For both together, explore the Deluxe setup.

What does installation cost?

Clear prices before work begins.

Pure Plus is $1,280 installed, Pure Premium $1,590 and Pure Luxe $2,290. Pure Home starts at $3,850 and Pure Home Reserve at $5,150. Prices include GST. We confirm your installation and any site requirements in writing.

How does lifetime cover work?

Covered for life while you stay serviced with us.

The sealed system and covered replacement labour are included while your paid annual service plan stays active. Valves and the filter tap have two years of cover. Pure Luxe electronics have five years, with separate terms afterwards. Cover applies to the original client at the installation address. Read the full warranty.

What is the difference between Pure Plus, Premium and Luxe?

Pure Plus is five stage alkaline reverse osmosis. Pure Premium is seven stage hydrogen rich alkaline reverse osmosis. Pure Luxe adds smart purity monitoring in a five stage alkaline system.

What makes Pure Home Reserve different?

Pure Home Reserve combines whole house chlorine and chloramine reduction with a stainless steel cabinet in 22 colour finishes. It starts at $5,150 installed including GST. We confirm the finish and site requirements in your quote.

Can I combine whole house filtration and reverse osmosis?

Yes. Deluxe includes Pure Home, Pure Premium reverse osmosis and a premium three way mixer, from $6,290 installed including GST. The first year of servicing is included.

Do I need a separate filtered tap?

Choose a dedicated filtered water tap or a three way mixer. A three way mixer delivers hot, cold and purified water through one fixture, with a separate filtered water path.

Does the lifetime warranty cover everything?

It covers the sealed system and covered replacement labour while your paid annual service plan remains active. Valves, tapware and smart electronics have separate limits. Cartridges and routine replacement parts are separate. Read the cover and conditions.
