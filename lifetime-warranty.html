Lifetime Warranty
On the sealed system, labour included

"If the system itself ever fails — cracks, leaks, faults — we replace it and do all the labour, free, for life. The wear parts around it, we look after at your yearly service."

The value isn't a cheap part. It's the two hours of a licensed plumber — shutting off, draining, breaking it down and re-plumbing — that you never pay for. That's the promise with teeth.

What's covered

Three layers of cover, so nothing gets forgotten

Every system we install is protected on three levels — the body of the system for life, the valves for two years, and the everyday wear parts at a fair fixed price after that.

Lifetime · Free

The sealed system + all the labour

The housings, RO membrane housing, centre joiner and Quick Change manifold — the body of your system. If any of it fails through a fault or normal wear, we replace it and do the full plumbing job to swap it out, at no cost, for as long as you own the system and stay on your plan.

Covers: housings · RO housing · centre joiner · manifold & smart internals · 100% of the labour
2 Years · Free

The valves

The pressure limiting valve, check valve, shut-off valve, feedwater valve and filter tap are all covered free for the first two years, backed by our supplier's own manufacturer warranty. If one fails in that window, we replace it, no charge.

Covers: PLV · check valve · 4-way shut-off · feedwater valve · filter tap
Fixed low price

The everyday wear parts

After two years, valves and other wear parts are replaced at a fixed, published, low price — because we're already on-site for your service. No made-up-on-the-day numbers. A valve is $80 done, a tap $170, a storage tank $240. Fair, and no surprises.

No call-out fee when done during your annual service

A note on Pure Luxe smart electronics: the smart monitoring, sensors and display on the Pure Luxe are fully covered for the first 5 years. After that, if the electronics ever fail, we replace the entire system and do the install labour free — you simply pay 50% of a new system as a fixed $600 contribution. It keeps the smartest system in the range protected without an open-ended promise we couldn't honestly keep.

How it stays in force

Three simple things keep it valid

The lifetime warranty is a benefit of an ongoing relationship, not a sticker on a box. Keep these three true and you're covered for life.

1

We installed it

The system was bought and installed through Filters For You, not a retail kit we were asked to fit.

We service it every 12 months. There's a 30-day grace period on each service, so a busy month is never a problem.

3

The system stays at its original address, and it's only ever opened or worked on by us.

✓

Do those three and the sealed system and its labour are yours, free, for the life of the system.
