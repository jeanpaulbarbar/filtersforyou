How reverse osmosis works

Five stages between the mains and your glass.

Stage 01

Sediment

A five micron cartridge catches the dirt, grit, rust and sand first, so nothing downstream has to.

Stage 02

Carbon block

A five micron coconut carbon block takes out the chlorine and chemicals, and the taste and the smell go with them.

Stage 03

The membrane

The core of the system. A 50 GPD membrane so fine that fluoride, PFAS, microplastics, metals and salts cannot get through. Up to 99% out.

Stage 04

Alkaline

Then the good minerals go back in. Magnesium and calcium, so the water tastes the way water should.

Stage 05

Carbon polish

One final pass through coconut carbon, right before the tap, so every glass is crisp and clean.

What reverse osmosis removes, and what it does not

Our reverse osmosis systems remove up to 99% of dissolved contaminants. The membrane works down to about 0.0001 micron, which rejects fluoride, heavy metals, nitrates, PFAS and dissolved salts, and helps remove microplastics. Sydney's water is safe to drink. Sydney Water publishes what is in the supply, and it is treated for a whole city. Reverse osmosis is for what is left once it is safe: the taste, and the dissolved things a carbon filter alone does not touch.

One honest line. The membrane doesn't take chloramine out. The carbon does. Chloramine is the disinfectant Sydney Water uses across most of the city, and for that the answer is whole house filtration at the mains. Plenty of homes run both: the whole house system for every tap and shower, and a reverse osmosis system for the water they drink.

How a reverse osmosis water filter works, stage by stage

Mains water comes into the cabinet and passes through the stages in order. Five stage reverse osmosis. That's purified water on tap. No more bottles coming through the front door.

  • Sediment. A five micron spun cartridge takes the grit, rust and sand out first.
  • Carbon block. Coconut carbon takes the chlorine, and with it the taste and the smell.
  • The membrane. Water is pushed under pressure through a membrane with openings around 0.0001 micron. Fluoride, PFAS, microplastics, dissolved metals and salts are rejected and flushed to the drain. The purified water is stored in a small pressure tank beside the system.
  • Alkaline. The good minerals go back in on the way to the tap. We remineralise, because minerals are good water.
  • Carbon polish. One last pass before the tap.

Pure Premium adds two stages after the membrane, a hydrogen rich alkaline stage and a ceramic far infrared stage, which is where its seven come from. Pure Luxe runs the five in quick change cartridges inside a compact sealed case, with a booster pump, a leak sensor and a screen that shows the purity live.

What does reverse osmosis remove?

Our reverse osmosis systems remove up to 99% of dissolved contaminants. The membrane works down to about 0.0001 micron, which rejects fluoride, heavy metals, nitrates, PFAS and dissolved salts. The membrane leaves the disinfectant to the carbon, and for that you want the whole house system at the mains.

Does RO remove the good minerals too?

No. The membrane does the deep separation first, then the system puts minerals back. Pure Plus finishes alkaline, Pure Premium adds hydrogen richness and fuller remineralisation, and Pure Luxe tracks it live on the screen. Minerals are good water. We take the contaminants out, not the good part.
